how it works

the device hash explained

instead of a user account, voidspace uses a device hash as a privacy-preserving identity anchor. here is exactly how it is created:

Android: we read your device's ANDROID_ID, combine it with a secret salt baked into the app at build time, and run it through SHA-256. the result is a 64-character hex string. we never store or transmit ANDROID_ID itself.
iOS: we read identifierForVendor (a value Apple generates per app-vendor pair), combine it with the same salt, and run it through SHA-256. the result is stored in the iOS Keychain, where it survives app reinstalls.

the hash is a one-way function. it cannot be reversed to identify your device or your person. it is used solely for moderation: rate limiting posts, preventing duplicate likes, and enforcing bans on devices that repeatedly violate the content policy.

important limitation: a factory reset on Android generates a new ANDROID_ID, which produces a new device hash. this means a ban can be bypassed by factory-resetting the device. we acknowledge this limitation openly — enforcing bans without collecting personal data involves an unavoidable tradeoff.
optional

push notifications

if you allow notifications, your device's push token is stored in our database. it is linked only to your device hash — not to any personal identity. we use it to deliver two types of notifications:

  • ❤️ someone liked your post
  • 💬 someone replied to your post or group message

we do not send marketing notifications. you can revoke notification permission at any time in your device's settings, which stops all future notifications immediately.

the void

what voidspace is for

voidspace exists for honest, anonymous expression — thoughts you can't say on a platform where your name is attached. that freedom is the whole point, and it depends on everyone using it in good faith.

the rule of thumb: say what you actually think. share what you actually feel. the only posts that don't belong here are the ones that would harm someone else or violate the rules below.

how it works

the moderation pipeline

moderation works in three layers. no single layer is infallible, so they work together.

01
automated pre-screening
every post is scored by the Google Perspective API before it publishes. posts above a toxicity threshold of 0.75 are held in a pending review queue rather than rejected outright — borderline posts can be released if the score was wrong.
02
community reporting
any user can report a post or comment with one tap. three unique reports from different devices automatically hides the content pending review. reports are recorded once per device per piece of content — flooding a report button has no additional effect.
03
device restrictions
devices responsible for repeated or severe violations are restricted from posting and commenting. restrictions can be temporary (with a visible countdown) or permanent, depending on severity. the device hash is what is restricted — not an account or a person.
after a restriction

bans and appeals

if your device is restricted, you will see a clear message explaining:

  • 📋 the reason for the restriction
  • ⏳ how long the restriction lasts (or that it is permanent)
  • 👁️ that you can still read and like posts during a temporary restriction

if you believe your device was restricted in error, you can appeal by emailing policy@voidspace.app with a description of the situation. because there are no accounts, we identify appeals using the device hash you provide voluntarily. we review every appeal.

known limitation: a factory reset generates a new device hash. we acknowledge that this means bans can be bypassed. this is an inherent tradeoff of a privacy-first architecture that does not collect personal identity. we document this rather than pretend it does not exist.
groups

group-specific rules

all of the above rules apply inside groups. additionally:

  • 👤
    group admins are responsible for moderating their own groups. they can remove members and delete messages.
  • 🔑
    admins who create a private group are given a 12-word recovery phrase. this is shown once and never stored by voidspace. losing it means losing admin access if you switch devices.
  • 📆
    groups with no member activity for 42 days are permanently deleted along with all their messages and membership records.
